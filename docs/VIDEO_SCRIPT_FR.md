> In crypto, you think you own assets. With tokenized Real World Assets, you don't hold tokens. You hold counterparties. If the issuer halts redemptions, your entire portfolio is frozen, no matter what stock you think you own.

**Action écran :** Ne bouge pas, laisse voir le titre "You don't hold tokens. You hold counterparties."

### Bloc 2 [15-35s] — Survole le FUNNEL 8 → 6 → 3 + HHI Radar
> This is Underlying, the first portfolio tracker built on CoinMarketCap's RWA API. Look at this demo book. Eight positions that look diversified. Underlying collapses them through the issuer join. Eight wrappers become six real assets, held through only three issuers. Eighty-two percent of the tokenized book runs through a single issuer: Backed Assets. The HHI is six thousand nine hundred ninety-three. Deep in the critical zone.

**Action écran :** Passe la souris lentement sur le Funnel (8 wrappers → 6 underlyings → 3 issuers) puis sur la carte HHI Threat Radar à droite.

### Bloc 3 [35-55s] — Scroll vers Holdings, hover badge SEC CIK, puis Wrapper Arbitrage
> Every underlying is linked to its official SEC filing via its Central Index Key. Here, Nvidia, CIK zero zero zero one zero four five eight one zero. And when one company has three different wrappers, which one do you buy? Backed has sixty-eight million in volume. Ondo has three million. Dinari has no market at all. Buying the wrong ticker means you cannot exit.

**Action écran :** Scroll doucement vers le tableau Holdings → hover 1 seconde sur le badge violet "SEC CIK" → scroll vers la section "Which Wrapper Do You Own?"

### Bloc 4 [55-75s] — Clique sur Scan Wallet → Treasury Preset, puis Export CSV
> Underlying bridges TradFi and Web3. Scan any EVM wallet live for tokenized treasuries, or paste your spreadsheet via CSV. One click exports the full institutional audit report.

**Action écran :** Clique sur "🔍 Scan Wallet" → clique "Institutional Treasury" → ferme → clique "📥 Export CSV" (montre le téléchargement)

### Bloc 5 [75-90s] — Scroll tout en bas au TERMINAL CMC
> Underlying composes seven CoinMarketCap endpoints, from the twenty-five issuer universe to unified pricing for crypto and tokenized stocks. Check the live capability probe and API console. This is Underlying, built for Build with CMC.

**Action écran :** Scroll jusqu'au "CoinMarketCap Developer Terminal" → clique sur 2-3 onglets (Issuers List, Single Issuer, Quotes) pour montrer les payloads.
