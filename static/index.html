    RISK QUANTIFICATION

    Mathematical Framework & Methodology

    1. Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) for Counterparty Risk

    In traditional antitrust and financial regulation (US Department of Justice / Federal Reserve), HHI measures market concentration across participants:

    HHI = ∑ (si)2   [for i = 1 … N]

    where si is the percentage share (from 0 to 100) of total portfolio collateral held by custodian/issuer i.

    • < 1,500: Well-Diversified Portfolio (Unconcentrated).
    • 1,500 – 2,500: Moderate Concentration Risk.
    • > 2,500: Critical Counterparty Risk. In our default book, HHI reaches 7,041 because 83.9% of equity collateral is concentrated with a single Swiss SPV (Backed Finance AG).

    2. Basis Spread Calculation (BPS)

    Measures the pricing discrepancy between the secondary token wrapper traded on DEXes and the underlying stock's primary market price:

    Spread (bps) = ((Pwrapper − Pequity) / Pequity) × 10,000

    Negative basis (e.g. -14 bps) signals an illiquid secondary market trading at a discount due to exit friction.

    3. SEC EDGAR CIK Look-Through Verification

    Every token wrapper resolved by Underlying maps via CoinMarketCap's /v5/real-world-assets/info endpoint to the SEC Central Index Key (CIK) of the underlying company. This allows institutional compliance officers to jump directly to official 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K filings on the SEC's EDGAR database.

    DIRECT FEEDBACK FOR CMC PRODUCT TEAM

    CoinMarketCap API Engineering Review

    As requested in the hackathon brief ("Tell us where the API got in the way... that feedback goes straight to the product team"), here is our technical audit from building Underlying:

    🌟 1. What the CMC RWA API Made Possible ("The Breakthrough")

    The relational link between /v5/real-world-assets/issuers/list and /v5/real-world-assets/issuers is the cornerstone of Underlying. By mapping crypto_id → rwa_id + issuer_id, CoinMarketCap solved the hardest problem in RWA: mapping anonymous ERC-20 contract addresses to real corporate issuers and SEC CIKs. Furthermore, /v2/cryptocurrency/quotes/latest natively prices both Bitcoin and tokenised NVIDIA stock in the exact same payload.

    ⚠️ 2. Friction Point 1: 403 on Startup Tier for Orderbook Pairs

    The endpoint /v5/real-world-assets/market-pairs/list returns HTTP 403 Forbidden on the Startup tier plan granted to hackathon participants (it requires Scale/Enterprise). Because of this, developers cannot compute granular orderbook depth for RWA tokens.

    Recommendation for CMC: Expose basic 24h aggregate volume and primary DEX venue directly inside /v5/real-world-assets/info or /quotes/latest so Startup-tier apps can evaluate exit liquidity without a 403 error.

    ⚠️ 3. Friction Point 2: N+1 Traversal on Cold Boot

    Building the full universe of 25 issuers and 2,400+ token wrappers requires walking every issuer with pagination (~60 sequential HTTP requests). On a 30 calls/min rate limit, a cold start takes 120 seconds. We solved this by pre-baking a 354KB gzip seed cache (seed_cache.json.gz), reducing boot time to 5 milliseconds.

    Recommendation for CMC: Add a bulk endpoint GET /v5/real-world-assets/universe/all returning all active token-issuer mappings in a single compressed response.

    ⚠️ 4. Friction Point 3: Global Identifiers Beyond SEC CIK

    The cik field is fantastic for US public corporations, but tokenised European sovereign bonds, Asian equities, or private credit funds lack SEC CIKs.

    Recommendation for CMC: Include standardized ISIN (International Securities Identification Number) and LEI (Legal Entity Identifier) in /v5/real-world-assets/info to support truly global TradFi compliance.
