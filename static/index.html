You don't hold tokens.
You hold counterparties.

Every tokenised stock is a claim on an issuer, not a share in a vault. Underlying prices your book against live CoinMarketCap data and rolls it up past the ticker — to the underlying company (verified by SEC CIK) and the issuer who holds your collateral.
